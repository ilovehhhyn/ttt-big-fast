## 1. Bottom line

**The memory mechanism is supported. H1 itself is untested. The method's measured lead over a
fair baseline is small.**

The thesis inverts TTT-E2E on a pretrained Llama-3.2-1B with sliding-window attention: the fast
(test-time) weights are big, the full MLP matrices of the last 4 of 16 blocks (201,326,592
parameters), and the slow weights are small, a rank-64 LoRA plus norm gains and learned inner
learning rates. The LoRA is the slow weight set, meta-learned through the inner loop; it is not
a fine-tuning baseline with test-time training added on top (P17, "0.1 Hypothesis and arms"; R,
"Arm B - TTT-naive"; RUN, `ARMS`; R, "What the `--inner-lr 0` run is, and is not"). MEASURED at
32K on PG-19 with nothing trained,
TTT improves loss by +0.1405 nats per book (95% CI [+0.1279, +0.1531], positive in 22 of 22
books), all of it beyond the 8192-token window (R, "TTT alone (arm B against arm A), nothing
trained"). MEASURED on a trained arm C, switching the inner loop off on identical weights costs
+0.0248 nats per book (95% CI [+0.0194, +0.0301], 22 of 22 books) (R, "Same weights, inner loop
on and off").

Both intervals exclude zero, but the second effect, the one that isolates test-time training
inside the method, is small next to what fine-tuning does alone: a plain LoRA fine-tune with no
inner loop takes the loss from 3.7119 to 2.7125, and arm C at the same 10-step budget reaches
2.6690. R gives that lead as 0.044 nats; it rests on one pair of runs, with no interval and no
seeds (R, "Arm C at 32K"). H1's falsifier (arm C ≈ arm B while arm D ≫ arm B) cannot be
evaluated, because arm D has never been trained; arm F, the control that isolates fast-weight
size, is not implemented (R, "Runs in flight"; RUN, guard in `build_everything`). At 32K,
training so far is 8 to 10 outer steps, a budget R puts at roughly 300 to 600 times smaller
than the reference's, with a meta-gradient truncated to 2 of 32 inner steps; the un-tuned arms
A and B are outside the reference protocol (R, "Caveats"; R, "What TTT-E2E's protocol says the
baseline is").

The biggest open risk is that the 0.044-nat lead over the protocol-faithful baseline does not
survive once both sides are trained properly. The runs that would show this are PENDING, with
an estimated start of 2026-09-24 (R, "Runs in flight").

## 2. The thesis and how it was to be tested

**H1, verbatim** (P17, "0.1 Hypothesis and arms"): "On a pretrained sliding-window Transformer, a
small slow set (attention LoRA + RMSNorm gains + per-tensor inner learning rates) meta-learned
through the inner loop captures most of the gain that TTT-E2E's all-weights outer loop provides
over TTT-naive, while the fast weights are the model's own MLP matrices (initialized from
pretraining, reset every sequence). **Falsifier**: arm C ≈ arm B while arm D ≫ arm B."

The preprint puts the test as: "The hypothesis is supported if (C) approaches (D) and both
exceed (B); it is falsified if (C) matches (B) while (D) does not." (TEX, "Method", "Arms and
metrics"). RQB's version of H1 includes MLP LoRA in the slow set; see section 8, B6.

## 3. What has been done

**Model and data.** Llama-3.2-1B from `unsloth/Llama-3.2-1B` ("ungated mirror, verified identical
config and token ids"); the chunked prefix+suffix path reproduces HuggingFace logits with max
abs difference 0.0000 and correlation 1.000000. DCLM documents of >=8193 Llama-3 tokens: 199.6M
train tokens, 902K val tokens = 110 sequences of 8192; PG-19 for 32K, token counts not recorded
(R, opening paragraphs). Arm E is a third-party 760M checkpoint converted from orbax (344
tensors, 883.7M params); its interleaved RoPE convention alone was worth 2.0 nats (R, "Arm E").
An evaluation contamination was found and fixed: PG-19's validation split opens with the King
James Bible (0.19 nats, 1,039 distinct tokens over 7K positions), so the split is now shuffled
with a fixed seed (R, "Corrected 32K numbers").

**Inner and outer loop.** Chunked inner loop with the loss taken before the update (Eq. 6),
functional fast weights, a second-order path, fast weights reset every sequence (P18, "Core
mechanism"). Gates passed: inner lr = 0 reproduces the no-TTT gradient exactly; checkpoint group
size is numerically inert; gradients flow through the KV cache (F §11). At 1B scale the full
machinery with inner LR 0 gives 2.4939 against arm A's 2.4940 (R, "The three things this table
says").

**Memory work that made 32K fit.** At 8K the binding cost is the attention double backward
through the math SDPA backend: backward grows about 12 GiB per additional fast block, forward
under 2 GiB, and `fast_blocks=4` peaks at 77.30 GiB (R, "What makes fast_blocks=4 fit at 8K"). At
32K the frozen prefix is segmented with a rolling KV cache (exact; in one shot it holds 72 GiB)
(R, "The regime matters"). Two more fixes followed: truncated BPTT had been a no-op for memory
until each window was differentiated as soon as it closes (gradients unchanged, pinned by
`test_per_window_backward_matches_single_backward`), and each prefix segment is now checkpointed
individually. F §13 also records that an earlier probe was itself wrong and retracts two
conclusions of F §12.

| context | truncate_bptt | resident across sequence | peak | outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 16K | 0 (exact) | 7.11 -> 39.20 GiB | 77.45 | OOM in backward |
| 16K | 2 | flat 8.73 GiB | 55.39 | completes |
| 32K | 2 | flat 8.86 GiB | 49.57 | completes |
| 32K | 4 | - | 77.45 | OOM |

(F, "13. Why arm C really OOMed, and the two fixes".) The price: "arm C at 32K optimises a biased
objective". Arms A and B and all evaluation are unaffected (F §13 "Cost").

**Robustness and reproducibility** (F, "14. Cross-check against the reference implementation,
and robustness fixes").
- A warmup that rounds to zero is a hard error at config construction. Evaluation no longer
  builds a meta-gradient: 32K evaluation went from out-of-memory to 11.5 GiB peak.
- A non-leaf tensor shared by every truncation window was freed by the first window's backward
  (the prefix output, then `exp(inner_lr_log)`); both are now cut at a detached leaf, and step
  time went 455.8 -> 131.9 s/step. The tests are parametrised over optimizer x learned_lr and
  "were checked to fail with the cluster's exact error when each fix is removed".
- Exact checkpoint/resume, on by default. On real hardware (job 14174410) a run killed with
  SIGKILL resumed at step 4/10; against the uninterrupted `C_32k_q10` (job 14163396) the
  evaluation is 2.66892 against 2.66895. Whether the largest gradient-norm difference, 1.7% at
  step 8, is run-to-run noise is PENDING (`C_32k_q10_repeat`) (R, "Checkpoint / resume on real
  hardware").
- `--arm F` refuses to run rather than "report arm C's numbers under arm F's name" (RUN).

**Evaluation and statistics tooling.** Per-sequence losses in result files; `--eval-ttt-off`; a
document-clustered paired test (`ttt/eval/paired.py`, `scripts/paired_ttt_effect.py`, with
`--baseline` to pair two result files); a per-token-index breakdown; `scripts/compare_runs.py`; a
forgetting probe reachable from the CLI, "exercised only on SmolLM2-135M" (R, "Runs in flight").

**Tests.** F §11 records 140 tests on 2026-09-18. `pytest --collect-only` on this machine today
lists 180 cases (my sum of its per-file counts, `hf`-marked tests deselected). I did not run the
suite.

### 4.8 The inner learning-rate unit (MEASURED, confirmed against the reference config)

The reference inner rule is `clip_by_global_norm(1.0)` then `sgd(lr=1)`. In this codebase's
per-element RMS unit, a unit-norm step over n_fast elements is 1/sqrt(n_fast) = 7.05e-5 for
n_fast = 201,326,592. The plan derived its scale from the paper's 11.5M-parameter prime MLP and
was 14x too large. The unit "is now confirmed from their config rather than inferred"; the 32K
optimum is 4e-6, 0.057x theirs (F §14 "The inner-LR unit"). A per-element step "is NOT
transferable across fast-weight set sizes" (P18, "Correction"). Section 8, B7 records a
disagreement about when the reference update has norm exactly 1.

### 4.9 The loss cliff beyond the window (HYPOTHESIS, diagnostic PENDING)

MEASURED: arm A scores 2.33 inside the window and 4.13 to 4.21 beyond it. The plain LoRA
fine-tune scores 2.3754 in the window and about 2.83 beyond it: in-window slightly WORSE
(+0.05), beyond-window about 1.35 nats better. On that basis R retracts its earlier description

HYPOTHESIS, in R's words "NOT yet verified": a Llama pretrained with full attention breaks under

## 5. What this means for the thesis

**What supports big fast weights with small slow weights.**
- A 201,326,592-parameter fast set made of the model's own MLPs works as test-time memory with
  nothing trained. The gain is absent inside the window and grows beyond it (4.3, 4.4). This
  positional pattern is the cleanest evidence in the repository, because it is within-sequence.
- The same shape survives training a small slow set, on identical weights (4.6).
- Arm C leads the budget-matched plain fine-tune by 0.044 nats at 10 steps, and R notes the
  in-window band of those two runs "differed by -0.0006, i.e. nothing" (4.5, 4.6).
- At 8K, 18 outer steps of meta-learning recovered 38% of the damage naive TTT does (4.1).
- The correctness gates are strong (HF parity; lr-0 reproduction to four decimals).

**What cuts against it, or limits it.**
- On a fine-tuned model TTT contributes little: +0.0248 nats per book, against +0.1405 on the
  un-tuned model. A plain fine-tune with no inner loop reaches 2.7125 from 3.7119; arm C reaches
  2.6690. R is right that these do not decompose additively, but on my reading none of them
  makes test-time training the main source of arm C's improvement over arm A.
- The 0.044-nat lead is one pair of 10-step runs with no interval and no seeds. The only
  recorded run-to-run comparison is the resume check, same configuration on two nodes:
  evaluation 2.66892 against 2.66895. Variance across seeds is unmeasured (section 3; R, "Runs
  in flight").
- Training is short: 8 to 10 outer steps at 32K and 18 at 8K, where the plan called for
  250-step sweeps and 2600-step headline runs (4.10; P17, "0.2 Fixed values").
- The meta-gradient spans 2 of 32 inner steps and is biased. How much that matters is PENDING
  (`C32k_t1`) (R, "Caveats").
- Un-tuned arms A and B are outside the reference protocol. The +0.1405 headline is measured
  against a baseline that falls off a cliff at the window edge, for a reason that is still a
  HYPOTHESIS (4.9, 4.10).
- The 8K point of the context-scaling trend is confounded with inner LR and dataset (4.2).
- Where T = k the method has lost so far: arm C was +0.1052 worse than no TTT at 8K, and
  indistinguishable from arm B at reduced scale, both with the earlier slow set (4.1).
- AdamW, a required arm, recovers about a third of normalized SGD's gain as arm B, optimum
  unlocated, and has not been used in arm C (4.7).
- Numbers are on the project's own split with PG-19 in place of Books3: "not comparable to
  their published tables" (R, "Caveats"). Arm E is 8K only, third-party, not parameter-matched.
- Forgetting is unmeasured on the real model, although the design overwrites the pretrained MLP
  that the paper keeps static as "safe storage" (F §3 item 3; R, "Runs in flight").

**Relation to H1's falsifier.** Arm D has never been trained at any scale, so neither the
falsifier ("arm C ≈ arm B while arm D ≫ arm B") nor the support condition ("(C) approaches (D)
and both exceed (B)") can be evaluated. **The experiments needed to evaluate H1 have not been
run.** The C-versus-B half exists only in weak forms: C better than B by 0.0641 at 8K after 18
steps; C indistinguishable from B at reduced scale; and at 32K a raw gap (2.6690 against 3.5694)
that the control shows is mostly reachable by fine-tuning with no inner loop, so it is not
evidence about meta-learning.

**Also unknown.**
- Whether "big" matters. Nothing compares big with small fast weights under one regime: arm F
  is not implemented and the fraction ablation stops at 1/4.
- Whether arm C's lead grows, holds or vanishes with more steps or larger batches (PENDING).
- Whether meta-learning through the inner loop adds anything at 32K beyond fine-tuning plus
  naive TTT. My framing, not the docs': one of the four cells below is unmeasured, and no queued
